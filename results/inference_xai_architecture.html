🧬 Detailed Inference & XAI Architectural Data Flow

How raw clinical & omic data is processed, modeled, calibrated, simulated, and explained at the individual gene level

Stage 1: Linear Risk Projection
⚙️ Risk Score Formulation (Cox Elastic-Net Core) ηi = Xi,clin βclin + Xi,pca βpca
🏥 Clinical Inputs (Xclin)
Direct standardized clinical vector (Age, Sex, Tumor Stage, TMB).
ηclin, i = Σ Xi, j · βj
🧬 PCA Genomic Latent Vector (Xpca)
High-variance genes (300) compressed into 50 principal components.
ηpca, i = Σ Xi, k · βk    (k = 1...50)

Stage 2: Novelty Processing Core
🔬 Parallel Inference, Calibration & Explainability Engine ηi → Calibration • Simulation • XAI Back-Projection
📊 1. Isotonic Calibration

Non-parametric monotonic mapping from relative risk score η to absolute baseline-adjusted survival.

Algorithm PAVA (Pool Adjacent Violators)
Output P(S > t | ηi) for t ∈ {12, 24, 36, 60m}
P(S > t) = fiso, t(ηi)
Guarantees monotonic risk ordering
🎲 2. Monte Carlo Simulation Engine

Inverse transform sampling over cumulative baseline hazard Λ0(t) across 5,000 stochastic trials.

Draw U(k) ~ Uniform(0, 1)
Hazard Λtarget(k) = -ln(U(k)) / exp(ηi)
t(k) = Λ0-1(Λtarget(k))
Output: P10, P50 (Median), P90, RMST
🔍 3. Gene-Level XAI Back-Projection

Mathematical novelty: Unrolls 50 PCA component weights back into 300 individual raw gene contributions.

Weight Vector Wgene = V · βpca ∈ &mathbb;R300
Gene Impact Δηg, i = Zg, i · Wgene, g
V = PCA Loadings (300 x 50)
βpca = Model Weights (50 x 1)
